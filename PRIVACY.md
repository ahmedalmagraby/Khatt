# Privacy Policy for Khatt

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Khatt is a browser extension that changes how Arabic-script text looks on web pages: its font, spacing and direction.

## What Khatt collects

Nothing. Khatt has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services. It makes no network requests of its own. The developer never receives any information about you or the pages you visit.

## What Khatt does on web pages

To find Arabic text, Khatt looks at the text of the pages you open. This happens entirely inside your browser. Page content is never stored, copied or sent anywhere.

Khatt changes only how text is displayed. If you switch on "Hide diacritics" or numeral conversion, the text shown on the page is rewritten in place and restored when you switch the option off. Text fields and editors where you type are never rewritten.

## What Khatt stores

Khatt stores only your own settings:

- **Your display choices** — font, text size, line height, word spacing, direction fixing, reading aids.
- **Sites you have customised** — the hostnames (for example `example.com`) of sites where you turned Khatt off or gave it different settings. Sites you merely visit are not recorded.
- **The name of an installed font**, if you choose to use a font from your own computer.

Display choices and customised sites are saved with your browser's built-in settings sync. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Google stores them with your account so they follow you to your other devices, under Google's own privacy policy. If sync is off, they stay on your device. The installed-font name is always kept on the one device only.

## Sharing

Khatt does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.

## Your control

- Remove any customised site, or all of them, from the Khatt options page.
- Export your settings to a file, or replace them from a file, on the options page.
- Uninstalling Khatt deletes everything it stored.

## Changes to this policy

If Khatt's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the date above changed before the new version is released.
